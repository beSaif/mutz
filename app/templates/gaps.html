How a lead becomes a data point

At triage, each need is saved as a field, its coverage and the archives searched. When the lead closes, its outcome is added.

What is never logged

Company names, the one pager text, the CEO's emails. Only the field, the coverage and the outcome.

Archive gap or Bern gap?

An empty pot can mean the expertise sits on a site we do not crawl. Check the archives before anyone draws a policy conclusion.

Share = leads with an empty pot for a field ÷ all leads that year. The count is always shown next to the percentage.
