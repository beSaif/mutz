Kestrelyn Health Oy

The company
Kestrelyn Health Oy was founded in 2021 in Tampere, Finland, and employs 26 people. It builds Kestra, a thin skin patch that continuously measures interstitial glucose and lactate in women during endurance sport. The patch pairs with a phone app that adapts training load and carbohydrate intake in real time. A first version is used by 400 amateur athletes in a pilot study in Finland and Sweden.

We are passionate about empowering every woman to reach her full potential, and we believe Kestra will revolutionise women's health. Our world-class team combines Nordic design with cutting-edge science.

Why Switzerland
The company is comparing Switzerland, Germany and the Netherlands for a European R&D and clinical base, to open in 2027. Its next product, for women who have type 1 diabetes, needs clinical evidence and a regulatory pathway in Europe.

Financials
Revenue 2025: EUR 1.4 million. Shareholders: founders 58%, a Nordic seed fund 31%, employees 11%. Current valuation: EUR 22 million.

What the company is looking for
- Clinical validation of the patch with women who have type 1 diabetes, including how glucose and lactate change across the menstrual cycle and during exercise.
- A research partner in biosensors, signal processing and machine learning to improve the glucose and lactate algorithms.
- Access to a clinical trial unit that can run a GCP compliant medical device study for CE marking under the EU MDR.
- Cleanroom and microfabrication capacity for prototyping the next generation of microneedle sensors.
- Non-dilutive funding for a joint R&D project with a Swiss research partner, for example through Innosuisse.
- Licensing options for university technology in sweat-based or microneedle sensing.
- A contract manufacturer for flexible electronics and patch assembly in Switzerland.
- Hiring around 10 people in the first two years: firmware engineers, data scientists and regulatory affairs specialists.
- About 400 m2 of lab and office space close to a university hospital.

Timeline
The location decision is expected in Q2 2027. The CEO plans to visit two or three Swiss cantons in spring.
