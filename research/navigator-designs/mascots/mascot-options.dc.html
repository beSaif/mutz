Who guides the Navigator?

10 Bern born sidekicks for the Research & Expertise Navigator

Hack Apertus · Liebefeld 2026
01
The Bern bear

Bärni

Bern's own bear, armed with a compass. Points every company to the right lab, professor or supplier in the canton.

“I know a guy. Actually, I know 300.”
02
Bernese mountain dog

Senni

A born retriever. Fetches facts from scattered websites and drops them at your feet, sources attached.

“Retrieval augmented good boy.”
03
Zibelemärit onion

Zibeli

Star of Bern's onion market. Peels a company brief layer by layer until the perfect match shows up.

“No tears. Just insights.”
04
Zytglogge clock tower

Zyti

The old town's famous clock tower. Rings the bell when the right expert meets the right company at the right time.

“Tick tock, your match is ready.”
05
Aare swimmer

Ottilia

Floats down the turquoise Aare like every Bernese summer. Goes with the flow of sources, never against it.

“Relax. I'll float you to the answer.”
06
Swiss cow matchmaker

Muuhtch

Rings her bell every time a startup clicks with a professor. Matchmaking, but make it alpine.

“It's a match! Muuuuh.”
07
Emmental mouse

Lochi

Lives in a block of cheese from the Emmental, right here in canton Bern. Spots the holes in your research and fills them.

“Every hole is a lead.”
08
Berner Rösti

Röschti

Crispy outside, soft inside, egg on top. Turns raw shredded research into a golden teaser a CEO reads in five minutes.

“Shredded facts, served hot.”
09
Alphorn herald

Alpi

The loudest thing in the Alps. Blows Bern's pitch across valleys and oceans, straight to CEOs abroad.

“Toooooot! Come to Bern!”
10
Fondue caquelon

Fondü

Melts scattered websites, papers and profiles into one smooth dip. Every single bite comes with a source.

“Drop your bread, buy the next round.”
