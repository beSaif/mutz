Veyra Vision ApS

The company
Veyra Vision ApS was founded in 2022 in Aarhus, Denmark, and employs 18 people. It builds Iris One, a handheld eye camera that screens for glaucoma and macular degeneration in a pharmacy or GP practice in two minutes. An AI model reads the retina images and refers patients who need an eye doctor.

We are on a mission to end preventable blindness, and we believe Iris One will change eye care forever.

Financials
Revenue 2025: EUR 0.9 million. Seed round of EUR 5 million closed in February 2026.

What the company is looking for
- A clinical study partner in ophthalmology at a university hospital, to validate glaucoma screening against a full eye exam.
- A research partner in medical image analysis and machine learning for retina images.
- Non-dilutive funding for a joint project with a Swiss research partner.

Timeline
The company wants to open a Swiss office in 2027 and is talking to two cantons.
